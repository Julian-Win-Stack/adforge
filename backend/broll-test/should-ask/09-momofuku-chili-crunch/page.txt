What it tastes like
Chili Crunch Sauce takes the flavor that started it all and puts it in a format made for everyday cooking. The same chili-forward, deeply savory profile — now in a rich, smooth sauce built to squeeze, spread, and finish. Miso and tamari add depth and umami while garlic layers in complexity. The heat is bold enough to be the reason you reached for it, without masking what's underneath.
