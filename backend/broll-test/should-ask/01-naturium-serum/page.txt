Frequently Asked Questions
What are exosomes in skincare?​
Naturium’s Multi-Active Exosome Serum features a vegan microalgae-derived biomimetic exosome, a plant-derived extracellular vesicle inspired by the skin’s natural renewal process. It’s formulated to help improve the look of skin firmness, elasticity, and resilience, working with your skin to support the appearance of a visibly renewed, more supple complexion.
What is the science behind exosomes as a skincare ingredient?
The science behind our Multi-Active Exosome Serum is built on testing and the results speak for themselves. Our microalgae-derived biomimetic exosome is backed by in vitro and in vivo testing, as well as clinical and consumer perception studies.
What makes our exosome ingredient different/is this Vegan?​
Yes. The Multi-Active Exosome Serum is vegan and cruelty-free. Our serum uses a microalgae-derived biomimetic exosome, inspired the skin’s natural renewal process.
How does a “biomimetic exosome” work on skin?
Our micro-algae biomimetic exosomes are plant-derived extracellular vesicles that work to help skin look visibly renewed, firmer and more supple. Hexapeptide-9, a biomimetic peptide, helps to improve the look of fine lines and wrinkles while antioxidants help to improve skin’s overall brightness and radiance.
How long until I see results from using Naturium's Multi-Active Exosome Serum?
In a consumer study of Naturium’s Multi-Active Exosome Serum:
•	91% agreed that skin instantly looks visibly renewed in a single use
•	94% saw a reduced look of fine lines and wrinkles in only 30 days of daily use
You may notice an immediate difference in how your skin looks and feels after the first application. With consistent daily use, the benefits continue to build improving the look of skin firmness, elasticity, and resilience over time, while skin appears calmer, more youthful-looking, and more supple with every use.
As with any skincare ingredient, individual results will vary based on skin type and routine consistency.
