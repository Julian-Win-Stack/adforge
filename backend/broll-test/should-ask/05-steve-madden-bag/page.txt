clutch
featuring a sleek metal frame and an elegant stone kiss-lock detail. This compact design combines timeless style with practical functionality, perfect for evening occasions or everyday elegance. The metal frame provides structure and durability, while the decorative stone accent adds a touch of luxury to your accessory collection.
Clutch bag

Care Instructions
How to Care for Textile/Fabric Shoes & Accessories
Wipe off dust with a soft dry cloth or soft brush. Use a soft cloth dampened with soapy water to gently scrub the fabric using small circular motions. Focus on stained or dirty areas.
Allow the surface to air dry at room temperature. Stuffing with paper can help maintain the shape while drying.
Do not use bleach, harsh chemicals, or direct heat, and avoid putting fabric items in the dryer, as this may damage the material or cause shrinkage.
Our care suggestions are provided as general guidance. We’re not responsible for any damage resulting from their use.
