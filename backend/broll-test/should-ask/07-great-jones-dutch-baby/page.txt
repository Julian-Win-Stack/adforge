What does the bottom of the lid look like?  Does it have "nipples" (that help drip steam on the inside)
I have this question too
2
people voted yes
12
people voted no
GJ
Great Jones
Answered by
Answered
almost 4 years ago
Store answer
The Dutchess lid is made with intentional "feet" which allows it to sit a bit raised on the base. These are not air bubbles, but are three small raised bumps along the edge, meant to let a bit of steam escape. Though these raise the lid slightly, the lid will still sit flush with the base. These are easy to identify as there are three and they are located on the underside rim of the lid.
