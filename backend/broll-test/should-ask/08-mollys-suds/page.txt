Made with 98% plant-based ingredients,
Molly's Suds Toilet Bowl Cleaner
removes stains, mildew and odors where you do your business. No ammonia, chlorine, or synthetic dyes & fragrances - only a sparkling clean toilet and bright citrus aroma to perk up your bathroom break.
Powered by clean ingredients for your health and home
Fights toilet bowl rings and removes stains from limescale, hard water & rust
Clinging gel formula free from ammonia and chlorine bleach
Extended reach neck provides 360 degree coverage under rim
Naturally scented with citrus essential oils (ISO-16128 compliant)
Septic safe
How to Use

For a spotlessly clean toilet bowl:
Step 1.
Point nozzle under rim of toilet bowl and squeeze gently.
Step 2.
Scrub with a toilet brush. Let sit for several minutes.
Step 3.
Flush and breathe a sigh of relief (don't be scared!) - your toilet bowl now looks (and smells) as good as new.
PRO TIP:
Use every 2-4 weeks to keep your toilet shining bright like you.
We adhere to strict ingredient integrity, so you’ll never find harmful chemicals in our products. Molly’s Suds Toilet Bowl Cleaner contains the following simple ingredients:
Water
