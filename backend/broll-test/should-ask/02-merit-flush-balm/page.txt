FAQs
What is Flush Balm by MERIT?
A lightweight cream blush that melts into skin for lit-from-within color with a natural-looking finish.
How do I use Flush Balm?
Apply to cheeks for a soft, buildable flush and tap onto lips for a hint of color.
What finish and coverage does it give?
Lightweight and blendable with a satin, natural finish that builds from sheer without looking heavy or cakey.
Is it safe for sensitive or acne-prone skin?
Yes—it’s fragrance-free, formulated without acne-triggering ingredients, and safe for sensitive and acne-prone skin.
What are the key ingredients?
Vitamin E to condition skin, microfine pigment powders for natural-looking color, and octyldodecanol for smooth, even application.
Is Flush Balm vegan and cruelty-free?
Yes—it’s Clean at MERIT and EU-compliant, vegan, and Leaping Bunny–certified.
What shades are available?
A curated range for fair to rich skin tones, including soft peach, cool mauve, sheer red, warm taupe, bright pink, and more.
How long does it wear?
The lightweight, buildable formula leaves a long-lasting glow that stays flush all day.
Is the packaging recyclable?
The component uses 43% recycled plastic with a 30% PCR cap. Our signature packaging is recyclable, reusable, or compostable, and the unit carton is FSC-certified.
