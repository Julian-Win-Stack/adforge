Question C8, cream or liquid behaves like the real thing: does any cream, gel, foam, liquid or powder in this clip look and behave like the real product?

How to look: follow the substance from where it comes out to where it ends up. Every change in it needs a visible cause: a hand, a squeeze, a tilt, a pump, a spray, gravity, or something touching it.

Fail only when the substance does something the real thing can't do, not when it just looks a bit off:
- it pours or spreads like the wrong material, or has clearly the wrong colour compared with the real product's photos;
- it appears or disappears without being applied, poured or wiped;
- it ignores the hand or surface touching it;
- it keeps flowing but the amount where it lands doesn't grow;
- it flows while nothing that makes it flow moves: the hand and container stay frozen, as in a photo.
Real product behaviour that looks odd passes (some serums are stringy, some foams are stiff).
"doesnt_apply" when no cream, gel, foam, liquid or powder appears.
