Question P4, staged like real life: is the scene set up the way this is really done?

First state, in one sentence, what the spoken line says is happening with the product: shown to the viewer, or used (worn, applied, poured, cleaned with, filled, opened, slept in, paired with an outfit...), on what or whom. If the line says or implies the product is in use or worn, the picture must set up that use; just holding the product up near the target is the wrong kind of action.

Then check against it, in this order, and say what you find for each:
1. Body position: is the person placed the way this is really done (for example lying, sitting, standing; facing which way; on, in or under the product when the product is something you lie on, sit in, wear or cover yourself with)?
2. Which hand and grip: is the product held in a hand that can do the action, with the grip people really use for an object of this size and weight? A grip that only exists for a camera fails: the product pinched by its very tip, corner or end with the fingertips while the rest of it sticks out unsupported, balanced on fingertips, or held in a way that would drop it or not let it be used.
3. Which part of the product: is the action set up on the part of the product it is really done with (the right slot, pocket, strap, opening, button, side, end)? Compare with the real product photos: something going into a product goes into the place made for that thing.
4. The target: is it set up on the right thing (the right body part, surface, item, person)?

Fail when any of these is the wrong kind (Rule B): wrong body position for the action, a product that should be in use or worn but is only held, a grip no one uses, the wrong part of the product, or the wrong target.
Pass when it is the right setup, even if imperfect: a normal hand around the product, showing it to the camera when the line only shows it, a slightly awkward but real pose.
"doesnt_apply" when no person or hand is in the picture and the line shows the product alone (the product alone on a surface).
