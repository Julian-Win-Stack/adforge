Question C7, used the right way, on the right target: is the product used the way it is really used, on what it is really used on?

First state, in one sentence, the action the spoken line needs. Then check what the clip shows against it:
- the kind of action (for example pressing, pouring, sliding in, applying) is how this product is really used;
- the part of the product used is the right one (the right slot, button, opening, side, handle within its real range of movement);
- the target is the right thing (the right body part, surface, object).

How to look: follow the product, the part being used and anything it goes into from the first frame to the last: where it goes, what it touches, and how far it moves. Don't judge from the start and end alone: a part that goes past where the real one can go at any moment, even if it comes back, or an item that ends up in a different opening or part than the action needs, is the wrong use.

Fail when the kind of action, the part of the product, or the target is wrong (Rule B), when the product is made to do something it physically can't (a handle pushed past its stop), or when the action isn't really done: the hand and the product stay frozen, as in a photo, while only the result moves.
Pass when it is the right use, even if done imperfectly. "doesnt_apply" when the product isn't used in the clip.
