Question C7, used the right way, on the right target: is the product used the way it is really used, on what it is really used on?

First state, in one sentence, the action the spoken line needs, and what state the product must be in before that action can really happen (for example opened, unlocked, switched on, a part moved to its real starting position). Then check what the clip shows against it:
- the starting state: the product is in that state before the action (a packet poured is open; a cap is off before anything comes out; a moving part starts where it really rests before it is moved);
- the kind of action (for example pressing, pouring, sliding in, applying) is how this product is really used;
- the part of the product used is the right one (the right slot, button, opening, side, handle within its real range of movement);
- the target is the right thing (the right body part, surface, object).
Fail when the starting state, the kind of action, the part of the product, or the target is wrong (Rule B), or the product is made to do something it physically can't (a handle pushed past its stop, contents coming out of a closed or sealed part, a strap, lid or handle swung along a path that would pass through the hand, body or another solid thing in the way). For a moving part, follow its path frame by frame: where it starts, where it ends, and what lies between.
Fail too when the spoken line says the product is being used or how it is used, and the clip has no talking presenter, yet the product is only shown or held and never used: the clip doesn't show the use the line needs.
Pass when it is the right use, even if done imperfectly. "doesnt_apply" when neither the clip nor the spoken line involves using the product (a presenter holding it while talking, or the product shown alone with a line that doesn't describe using it).
