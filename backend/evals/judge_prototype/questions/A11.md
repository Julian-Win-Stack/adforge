Question A11, same product in every scene: is it the same product in every scene of the ad?

Compare the product in each scene's still with the others and with the real product photos. Fail when the product in one scene is a different product, or a different model, colour or variant from the others, or a clearly different size from the others next to the hands and things around it, or when what it is used with changes between scenes in a way that makes it a different product.
Before judging size, think about the camera in each scene: a product held close to the camera looks bigger, one further away looks smaller, and the camera angle changes how big it looks. That is not a size change. Fail for size only when, after allowing for the camera, it is still clearly a different size next to the hands holding it or the things beside it.
Packaging in one scene and the bare product in another passes. A scene with no product in it is skipped.
"doesnt_apply" when the product appears in fewer than two scenes.
