Question A11, same product in every scene: is it the same product in every scene of the ad?

Compare the product in each scene's still with the others and with the real product photos. For each scene, note its model, colour, shape and its size against what is next to it (hands, face, body, furniture). Fail when the product in one scene is a different product, or a different model, colour or variant from the others, or when its size against the hands or body is clearly different from the other scenes (for example it fills the hand in one scene and is half that size in another), or when what it is used with changes between scenes in a way that makes it a different product.
Size judged against hands and body, not against the frame: a close-up or a far shot of the same-sized product passes.
Packaging in one scene and the bare product in another passes. A scene with no product in it is skipped.
"doesnt_apply" when the product appears in fewer than two scenes.
