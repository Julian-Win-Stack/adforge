Question C10, staged like real life: is the scene in this clip set up the way this action is really done, the whole clip through?

First state, in one sentence, the action the spoken line needs (what the person does with the product, to what). Holding the product up or showing it to the camera while talking is an action too: it is staged by how the product is held. Then check against it, in this order:
1. Where the product is: is it where it really is during this action? Something worn is on the body when the line is about wearing it, styling it or pairing it with clothes; something used on a surface is on that surface.
2. Body position: is the person placed the way this action is really done?
3. Which hand and grip: is the product in a hand that can do the action, held by the part and in the way people really hold it for this action (a full bottle held by its body or a sturdy handle, not pinched by a thin tip; a tool held by its handle)?
4. Which part of the product: is the action set up on the part it is really done with?
5. The target: is it set up on the right thing?
Fail when any of these is the wrong kind (Rule B), at the start or when it breaks during the clip.
Pass when it is the right setup, even if imperfect. "doesnt_apply" only when nobody holds, wears or uses the product in the clip (a person just talking with no product, or the product alone).
