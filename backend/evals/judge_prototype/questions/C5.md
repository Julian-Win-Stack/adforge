Question C5, real product including real size: is the product in this clip the real product from the shop's photos, the whole clip through?

Fail when, at any moment, the product is a different or invented product (wrong shape, parts, colours or materials), when it changes shape or details during the clip, or when its size is wrong next to the hands and things around it (for example a packet far bigger or smaller than the real one is next to a hand).
Pass when it stays the same real product at a believable size.
Printed letters and logos never fail, even when garbled, changing or missing. Packaging instead of the bare product passes when it matches the real packaging.
"doesnt_apply" when the product never appears in the clip.
