Question C5, real product including real size: is the product in this clip the real product from the shop's photos, the whole clip through?

What counts as the product here: the item itself and its packaging or container. Any cream, gel, foam, liquid or powder that comes out of it is checked by another question, so leave the substance out of this verdict.

How to check:
1. Go through every side and part of the product the clip shows: front, back, top, bottom, ends, handle, buttons, controls, vents, caps, attachments. For each one, find a shop photo that shows that same side and compare them. A side the photos show (even in only one photo) must match it; a part that looks different there fails, even when the overall shape and colour match. Examples: a grille or holes where the photo shows a button panel, buttons that aren't in the photos, a loop or handle in a different place or shape.
2. Compare readable printed words with the photos. Misspelled or garbled words fail.
3. Watch the whole clip: the product must not change shape, parts or details from one moment to the next.
4. Check its size next to the hands, body and things around it. A product far bigger or smaller than the real one would be next to a hand fails.

Fail when any of these is wrong at any moment. Pass when it stays the same real product at a believable size.
Letters too far away or too small to read pass. Packaging instead of the bare product passes when it matches the real packaging. A side or part no shop photo shows passes unless it plainly doesn't fit the rest of the product.
"doesnt_apply" when the product never appears in the clip.
