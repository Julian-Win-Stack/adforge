Question C5, real product, same size the whole clip: is the product in this clip the real product from the shop's photos, the whole clip through?

How to look, side by side, filling in the form as you go:
1. From the shop photos, write each side of the product they show (front, back, top, and so on) and every part on that side.
2. For every side of the product the clip shows, write its parts and the seconds, find the shop photo that shows that same side, and compare it part by part.
3. A part that is added, missing or different on any side fails. Don't stop at the overall shape and colours: check every side the clip shows.

Fail when, at any moment, the product is a different or invented product (wrong shape, parts, colours or materials), or when it changes shape, details or size during the clip (it grows or shrinks next to the hands and things around it, beyond what moving closer to or further from the camera explains).
Pass when it stays the same real product at the same size.
Printed letters and logos never fail, even when garbled, changing or missing. Packaging instead of the bare product passes when it matches the real packaging.
"doesnt_apply" when the product never appears in the clip.
