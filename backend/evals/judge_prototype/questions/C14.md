Question C14, the action really works: does the main action in this clip really work, and does its result stay?

First say in a few words what the main action is: what the product, or the tool or hand used with it, does to what (for example: a knife cuts a loaf, a brush paints a wall, a spout fills a cup, a hand screws on a lid). Then check step by step across all frames:

1. Set-up: the working part sits on what it works on the way that action needs, so the action could really happen. Say where the working part meets its target and in which direction it acts.
2. Result: a change the action makes (a cut, a painted strip, a stain gone, liquid in a cup) appears only where and when the working part reaches it.
3. Stays: once a result is made, follow that spot to the last frame. It must stay, unless something in the clip visibly undoes it.
4. Nothing on the action moves by itself: the product, the tool, the target and any piece being worked on move only when a hand, the tool or gravity moves them.

Fail when the set-up could not do the action, a result appears where the working part never reached, a result undoes itself, or a piece of the action moves by itself. Say which step and at which seconds. doesnt_apply when nothing in the clip acts on anything (the product is only held, shown, worn or talked about).
