Question P1, real product: is the product in this starting picture the real product from the shop's photos?

Fail when the product is a different or invented product: a wrong shape, wrong or missing parts, extra parts, wrong colours or materials, wrong printed words or logo where they can be read, or a wrong size next to hands and things around it.
Pass when it is the same product, even from another angle or partly hidden.
Letters too far away or too small to read pass: don't fail for blurry or unreadable text.
Packaging instead of the bare product (or the bare product instead of packaging) passes when it matches the real product's packaging or the real product.
