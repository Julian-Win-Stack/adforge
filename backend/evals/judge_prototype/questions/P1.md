Question P1, real product: is the product in this starting picture the real product from the shop's photos?

How to look, side by side, filling in the form as you go:
1. From the shop photos, write each side of the product they show (front, back, top, and so on) and every part on that side.
2. For every side of the product the picture shows, write its parts, find the shop photo that shows that same side, and compare it part by part.
3. A part that is added, missing or different on any side fails. Don't stop at the overall shape and colours: check every side the picture shows.

Fail when the product is a different or invented product: a wrong shape, wrong or missing parts, extra parts, or wrong colours or materials.
Pass when it is the same product, even from another angle or partly hidden.
Printed letters and logos never fail, even when garbled, misspelt or missing.
Packaging instead of the bare product (or the bare product instead of packaging) passes when it matches the real product's packaging or the real product.
