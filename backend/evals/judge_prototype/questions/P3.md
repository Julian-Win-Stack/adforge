Question P3, person not pasted onto the room: do the presenter and the room look like one real photo?

Fail when the person looks cut out and placed onto the background: lighting or shadows on the person that don't match the room, a hard outline or halo around them, wrong scale for the room, or the person not standing, sitting or leaning on anything the room has (feet or body not meeting the floor or furniture).
Pass when the person and the room look like one photo. "doesnt_apply" when no person is in the picture.
