Question P2, nothing impossible: does anything in this starting picture break physics?

Everything that is lifted, held or in the air needs a visible cause: a hand, a stand, or something it rests on.
Fail when, on the product, the hand, what it is used on, or the presenter: something floats with nothing holding it, passes through something solid, bends or melts where it is rigid, is joined to something it shouldn't be, has extra or missing fingers or limbs, or a copy of the product appears where only one should be.
Pass when everything could exist in a real photo. Flaws only in the background pass (Rule A).
