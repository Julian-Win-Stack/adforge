Question C6, nothing impossible: does anything in this clip move or change in a way that can't happen?

Fail when, on the product, the hand using it, what it is used on, or the presenter:
- something floats, passes through something solid (a strap through a leg), bends where it is rigid, or melts into something;
- a thing appears from nowhere or vanishes, or an extra copy of the product appears;
- a thing changes by itself mid-clip: a container refills itself, an item switches hands with no hand-over, a part changes position with nothing moving it, a body part changes;
- hands or fingers merge, multiply or bend in ways they can't.
Pass when every movement could happen in a real video. Flaws only in the background pass (Rule A).
