Question A12, presenter feels like a real person: do the presenter's face and voice in this talking clip feel like a real person's?

Watch and listen. Fail when the presenter feels fake: a flat, robotic or monotone voice with no natural expression; a voice that doesn't fit the face; lips that don't match the words; a face that is frozen, waxy, or moves in ways a face can't; eyes that don't blink or stare blankly; stiff body with only the mouth moving.
