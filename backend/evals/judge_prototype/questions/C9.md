Question C9, camera moves naturally: does the camera move the way a real camera could?

Fail when objects ride along with the camera as if stuck to the lens, the view warps or bends (straight lines curving, the room stretching), the camera zooms in and then back out for no reason, or the scene jumps to another viewpoint within the clip with no cut.
Pass when the camera is still, or moves smoothly as a person or a rig could move it.
