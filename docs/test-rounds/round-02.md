Details, in the same order:

### 1. B-roll that does nothing: a hand just holds, places or stands the product up (phone case scene 2; all 4 shower-cleaner B-rolls)

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 5 clips in 2 of 3 graded ads

- **Why it happened:** The round 1 structure fix made every middle scene B-roll; when the planner had no real action it was told 'Choose showcase when unsure', and showcase's example action was 'picked up or set down'. The fact-check rewrite also said 'show something else they support'.
- **What was changed:** Julian's rule 'The B-roll must do something. It must sell the product': showcase only when using or wearing it is the proof; a hand only holding/placing/standing up/pointing is never B-roll; a middle line must be proven by B-roll, else ask the shop owner, else it becomes a talking scene. (#121)
- **What happened next:** Round 3: no filler complaints; the bag's showcase clips (carried as a clutch, worn crossbody) all passed. Round 4: no filler complaint in 29 B-roll clips. On Nov 7 Julian went back to exactly this rule after trying a stricter 'proof' rule.

### 2. Toilet cleaner scene 4: the 'result' scene is the bottle beside an already clean toilet, no evidence it cleaned anything

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** Kind 'showcase' with shows 'the cleaner bottle rests beside a spotlessly clean toilet bowl': nothing told the planner a result has to be filmed happening.
- **What was changed:** Rule: a result is filmed happening, from before to after in the same clip; a clean or finished thing with the product beside it never shows a result. (#121)
- **What happened next:** Round 3 toilet cleaner scene 4 (brush scrubs the ring away) PASS. Round 4 toilet cleaner scene 4 (brush scrubs the rust stain away) PASS on his tap.

### 3. Phone case scene 5: the phone is dropped from just above the floor, so the drop proves nothing

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** No code kept the page's drop height: the start picture put the camera 'at knee height' with the phone 'above the floor', the 'six feet' claim had moved to the talking scene, and a shop photo of a drop, sent to the picture model as a 'need', was copied into the picture (this kind of photo leak was first seen Oct 7).
- **What was changed:** A drop's start picture shows the stated height (e.g. 6 ft), else a standing person's hand height, with the whole fall in view; photo 'needs' may name only how a thing looks, never an action or pose; a 7th question added to the start-picture check: 'makes sense for the line'. (#121)
- **What happened next:** Round 3: no drop-height complaint (phone case scene 3's complaint was the flip). Round 4 phone case scene 4 'drop-tested fifty times from six feet' PASS. Note: the 7th check was switched off the next day (12 false alarms, Julian Oct 10 03:10) and later replaced.

### 4. Shower cleaner: still no before/after photo, so the ad can't show the cleaning; 'the agent must ask'

**Known limit** · 1 ad (2nd round in a row)

- **Why it happened:** The app did ask the owner for a before/after photo; the stand-in owner had none (the shop's site has none), and the app then planned 4 filler scenes.
- **What was changed:** No app fix for the missing photo itself: Julian stopped testing the shower cleaner 'unless we are going to put in the real photos'. The filler part is fixed by the rule above; a drawn 'how it should look' picture approved by the owner went to ticket #119 (not built in this period).
- **What happened next:** Shower cleaner left out of rounds 3 and 4. The known-limits list records that Lemi Shine's real before/after demo lives in a YouTube embed the app can't download.
