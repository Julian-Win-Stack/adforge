# Round 5: 15 new products with the clip judge and auto-remakes (October 17–19, 2026)

## What this round tested

- 15 products the app had never seen, nothing faked: Glow Recipe serum, Rare Beauty blush, Olipop soda, Fishwife tinned salmon, Ember heated mug, HexClad pan, Rothy's flat shoes, goodr sunglasses, True Classic polo shirt, Gaiam balance board, Native Pet pumpkin powder (for pets), Cocolab floss, Blueland soap tablets, Loop earplugs, Dr. Squatch soap.
- First big run with the clip judge, automatic remakes of failed clips, and a new way of fixing from owner notes, all switched on.
- Graded by page taps, page notes and chat.

Details, in the same order:

### 1. Glow Recipe ad had no B-roll scene; every scene was the presenter talking ("Why no b roll scene?")

**Came back** · 1 of 15 ads

- **Why it happened:** The test runner, acting as the shop owner, declined the serum photo the planner asked for; the beauty rules then allowed no B-roll at all, so the planner made every scene talking.
- **What was changed:** The planner general rules (Oct 19) tried to stop a declined photo from wiping out all B-roll; the build notes say it still lost B-roll for Rare Beauty, and it was written down as a known limit. In round 6 the Glow Recipe ad was all talking again for a different reason (the judge failed every serum clip and the app swapped them to talking); that was fixed Oct 23 by keeping the last B-roll try instead of switching to talking. (#188, #208)
- **What happened next:** Round 6: Glow Recipe rerun was all talking again ('all talking, don't know why'), other cause. Round 6 Gymreapers also all talking (page reading).

### 2. Olipop scene 2: the B-roll invented a silly use (a fridge shelf slides out and the can slides over it); 'I wouldn't want this as a shop owner'

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 scene, 2 clips (first clip and kept remake)

- **Why it happened:** The plan filmed storing the drink, not using it, and 'slide forward' was read as toward the lens. The planner treats page words as licence and guesses how the product is used.
- **What was changed:** Planner general rules: storing, care and taking-out are not scenes; the action must be a real use. (#188)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Olipop rerun: scene 2 passed, ad passed.

### 3. Olipop scene 3: the woman's shirt changes colour partway through the clip

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The video model drifted from the starting picture (reference mode is only a looks guide). Julian later ruled the drift itself is not worth building a fix for.
- **What was changed:** Nothing built for the drift (dropped Oct 18 20:39). The judge was widened to look at clothes and body motion (see the judge-miss entry). (#186–#187)
- **What happened next:** Not seen again in round 6 grades (Olipop scene 3 passed).

### 4. The clip judge missed faults Julian saw: Olipop shirt colour change, Loop backwards ear ('the judge should have noticed it')

**Partly fixed** · 2 clips

- **Why it happened:** The judge was told to look only at the product and hands, so clothes were out of scope; the ear was already wrong in the starting picture, which the judge does not grade.
- **What was changed:** Judge reworded to a general motion scope (clothes, hair, body included) and the half-second forgive rule removed. (#186, #187)
- **What happened next:** Round 6: the judge still missed 5 of 14 clips Julian failed (mostly 'looks AI' motion).

### 5. Fishwife scenes 2 and 3 first clips: 'a little bit unnatural' (minor)

**Dropped** · 2 clips

- **Why it happened:** Small video-model motion flaws; the judge caught both.
- **What was changed:** No change needed: the app's own judge-and-remake loop replaced both clips in the same run and Julian passed the kept clips.
- **What happened next:** Kept clips passed in round 5.

### 6. Rothy's scene 3: shoes in a washing machine; all three tries bad

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 scene, 2 clips graded

- **Why it happened:** The plan asked the video model to invent the inside of a running washer; the washer filled a sixth of the frame; a 'same side faces camera' rule fought with tumbling.
- **What was changed:** Planner general rules: never film inside a running machine; care lines don't become B-roll. (#188)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Rothy's rerun: both B-roll scenes passed, ad passed.

### 7. True Classic scene 4: 'borderline, not catastrophic but not a great one'

**Not fixed** · 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** Not looked into; he named no fault.
- **What was changed:** No fix found.
- **What happened next:** True Classic was not in later rounds.

### 8. Native Pet scene 2: powder poured into empty space, then the scoop refills itself

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 scene, 2 clips (first and kept)

- **Why it happened:** A 1.5-second sprinkle stretched over a 7.3-second clip, and the video model redrew the first frame with the tub open, so it poured into the tub then refilled the scoop to do the ordered sprinkle. Across all 37 round 5 B-roll clips, one-moment actions failed and lasting actions passed.
- **What was changed:** Planner rule: the action must fill the clip by its own course; plus the fixing agent, which may change the action, picture and line after a judge fail. (#188, #191)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Native Pet rerun: no break ('Nothing broke, but not a great ad').

### 9. After a judge fail the app retried the same action and kept the newest fail (Native Pet kept a clip he failed over one he passed)

**Partly fixed** · 4 scenes (Native Pet scene 2, Cocolab scene 2 and scene 3, Blueland scene 2)

- **Why it happened:** Remakes only reworded the same action with more 'don't' words; the video model ignored the bans; on a tie the newest clip was kept.
- **What was changed:** Fixing agent: after the cheap reword, one agent owns the failed scene and may change the action, framing, picture, clip length and line, with positive orders only. (#191)
- **What happened next:** Round 6: fixing agent on 18 scenes: 7 passed, 4 failed, 4 gave up to talking, 3 killed by the restart of the background worker.

### 10. The judge was too strict: it failed clips Julian passed (Native Pet remake 1) and forced remakes that looked the same (Loop scene 3, Cocolab scene 4)

**Came back** · 3 clips

- **Why it happened:** 'Any 2 of 3 failing answers' counted two unrelated complaints as agreement.
- **What was changed:** A check fails only when two answers name the same fault at the same time (same-fault vote). Later (Oct 22) replaced by asking each check once. (#186, #187, #207)
- **What happened next:** Round 6: the judge wrongly failed 9 of 31 clips Julian passed; round 7 Beautyblender clips again failed by the judge that he called good.

### 11. Cocolab scene 2: the floss changes shape unnaturally and is not used the way floss is used

**Came back** · 1 scene, 3 clips

- **Why it happened:** The plan asked for a flossing action nobody does (one strand from one side, sawing across the front teeth); the video model can't keep a string whole.
- **What was changed:** Planner general rules (real use, filmable result) plus the fixing agent. (#188, #191)
- **What happened next:** Planner rules test: Cocolab flossing came back after 'go ahead without'. Round 6 Cocolab rerun failed: 'not how the product works... Every clip fails'.

### 12. Cocolab scene 3: a floss string appears from the hand and becomes two strings

**Came back** · 1 scene, 3 clips

- **Why it happened:** Same as above, plus the starting picture had floss in one hand only and the prompt never said how floss is held.
- **What was changed:** Same planner rules and fixing agent; starting pictures were later removed from B-roll altogether (Oct 29). (#188, #191, #210)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Cocolab still failed (scene 4).

### 13. Cocolab scene 3: the starting picture was wrong ('When the starting picture is not good')

**Came back** · 1 scene

- **Why it happened:** The picture check judges objects alone and never sees what the prompt asked for.
- **What was changed:** No new check (Julian: owner note drives a redraw through the fixing agent); later the B-roll starting picture was removed entirely. (#191, #210)
- **What happened next:** Round 6: wrong starting pictures in 8 of 19 scenes (Oct 24 count); one fixed by his note (KraveBeauty).

### 14. Cocolab scene 4: the refill shown is invented; the page only shows refilling in its video ('inventing the B-roll scenes is kind of bad')

**Came back** · 1 scene, 2 clips

- **Why it happened:** The planner sees only the page text and photos and guesses the usage.
- **What was changed:** Shop product videos now feed the planner (frames, transcript, one reader call). (#193, #194, #189)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Cocolab scene 4: 'not how the product works'. Round 7: invented usage again (Peace Out extractor tool, Dexas twisting).

### 15. The planner invents how the product is used (the 'new problem', across Olipop, Cocolab, Blueland)

**Partly fixed** · 3 of 15 ads

- **Why it happened:** Page words count as licence; photos of use are barred; the fact check compares words with words.
- **What was changed:** Planner general rules + product videos to the planner. (#188, #193, #194)
- **What happened next:** Fixed for Olipop and Rothy's in round 6; came back for Cocolab (round 6) and Peace Out, Dexas (round 7). Recorded in the video-model limits list as 'the most common fail in nearly every round'.

### 16. Blueland scene 2: a pipe sits in the bottle in the starting picture

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 scene

- **Why it happened:** Our own picture orders contradicted each other; the picture check never sees the prompt.
- **What was changed:** No new check; owner note through the fixing agent; later starting pictures removed from B-roll. (#191, #210)
- **What happened next:** Blueland made no ad in round 6 (photo colour rule), so not retested.

### 17. Blueland scene 2: liquid drips from the tablet for no reason, and a dropped tablet stays in the hand

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 scene, 2 clips graded (3 made)

- **Why it happened:** A half-second drop stretched over a 5.2-second clip; the hand held still for 4 seconds then a second tablet appeared.
- **What was changed:** Planner rule that the action fills the clip; fixing agent may change the action. (#188, #191)
- **What happened next:** Blueland made no ad in round 6.

### 18. Loop scene 2: the ear is backwards (start picture)

**Fixed (proven in a later round)** · 1 clip; also named in his whole-ad note

- **Why it happened:** An impossible ask: ear and plug visible but framed from the back; 'no face' meant no portrait.
- **What was changed:** Julian: fix upstream in how scenes are written, never by the judge; owner note through the fixing agent; later starting pictures removed. (#191, #210)
- **What happened next:** Round 6 Loop rerun ad passed with no complaint.

### 19. (Found by Claude, not raised by Julian) the price scene is cut to 0-2 seconds at the end of 11 of 15 ads

**Dropped** · 11 of 15 ads (2 at 0 s)

- **What was changed:** Nothing; Julian chose not to fix it.
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.
