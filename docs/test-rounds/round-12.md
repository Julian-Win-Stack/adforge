# Round 12: First round with the ad-quality rules (November 6–8, 2026)

## What this round tested

- New ad-quality rules: more B-roll (45% of ad time, up from 13%), speech planned at 2.8 words a second, a plan quality check, label and ready-product picture checks, and an end card on every ad.
- 8 round 11 products (all but SodaStream and Ooni) plus 4 new ones: a KONG dog toy, Crocs clogs, Gorilla Glue and the CATAN board game.
- Graded as whole ads, with a labeling page for quality.

Details, in the same order:

### 1. Hooks too long, not punchy, spoken slowly with low energy (round note; Revlon hook slow; BlenderBottle hook unclear)

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · All 12 ads (hook 'stops the scroll' = no on 12 of 12; named in words for Revlon and BlenderBottle)

- **Why it happened:** Hooks were 14-18 words (6-7 s). Nothing limited hook length or asked for energy; the voice words were the same calm 'conversational ad pace' for every clip, and a short hook would have been padded to the 12-word minimum line length.
- **What was changed:** The planner now writes a 12-word hook whose first sentence lands the problem or claim in about 8 everyday words, and scene 1's video prompt asks for an excited, high-energy delivery. Later, every line was rewritten in natural spoken style with delivery matched to each line. (#435, #434, #458, #463)
- **What happened next:** Free re-plan of round 12's 12 products: all 49 hooks exactly 12 words (before 14-18). No graded round since checks whether hooks now stop the scroll.

### 2. The presenter doesn't feel like a real person (flat, ad-read voice, still face)

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 12 of 12 ads (labeling page, Nov 7); every talking scene (25)

- **Why it happened:** Lines were page copy, voice words asked for an 'ad pace', 20 of 25 talking scenes told her to hold still, and none gave her a feeling to act. Creatify's prompts do the opposite (chatty voice, a feeling, a small product move, lip-sync line, close voice).
- **What was changed:** Talking scenes now use friend-style spoken lines, warm chatty voice words, one acted feeling per scene, a small product move on a word, natural body movement, a lip-sync line, quiet room sounds, and a steady phone. (#458, #459, #460, #462, #463, #464, #465, #466, #467, #468, #447)
- **What happened next:** Re-film test of 8 round 12 talking scenes with the new rules: Julian rated 8 of 8 'feels like a real person'; one new flaw (selfie camera shook) fixed and re-filmed 4 of 4 yes. Not yet tested in a full graded round.

### 3. No emotional 'after' moment (nobody shows relief, pride or a reaction)

**Not fixed** · 12 of 12 ads

- **Why it happened:** Nothing in the app asks for a reaction shot; ads go from features straight to price.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed yet (an AI check that reads the script for an 'after' moment was proposed and run as a measurement, not built)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 4. Talking scenes look too alike: only switching between selfie and front camera, the angle should change noticeably (Bondi had 3 talking scenes)

**Not fixed** · 1 ad (Bondi), 3 talking scenes

- **Why it happened:** A fact-check rewrite turned Bondi's middle scene into a talking scene without re-running the plan rules; talking scenes vary only the camera mode.
- **What was changed:** No fix found.
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 5. B-roll that means nothing / doesn't show real use (Bondi scene 2 only raises the capped bottle; Liquid I.V. scene 2)

**Partly fixed** · 2 ads, 2 B-roll clips

- **Why it happened:** Bondi: no photo of the mitt, so the planned mitt scenes were dropped and a fact-check rewrite made the B-roll 'raises the capped bottle' without checking the plan rules. The proof rule of round 12 also pushed plans toward claims a camera can't show.
- **What was changed:** Went back to the round 2 B-roll rule: every B-roll scene does something with the product that sells it; only holding or placing it isn't B-roll (except a still shot about fit or looks). The proof check was dropped. (#438, #437)
- **What happened next:** Free re-plan of round 12 products: hold/place-only B-roll 1 clear + 1 borderline (a Bondi foam dollop) vs 1 before; the rewrite path that made Bondi's scene was not changed. No graded round since.

### 6. Silent stall of 1-2 s at the end of a clip, no speech and no action (AeroPress scene 3)

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** She spoke fast (2.92 words/s), so the 7 s clip had about 1.8 s left; the prompt asked her hands to hold the pose; assembly played every clip whole.
- **What was changed:** Assembly now keeps at most 0.5 s of a clip after its last spoken word. (#446, #442)
- **What happened next:** Re-measured on round 12's clips: cuts with over 1 s of silence went from 4 to 2 (the 2 left are silence at the start of the next clip, left on purpose); the worst went from 3.8 s to 1.2 s. No graded round since.

### 7. Unnatural grip: product held by one finger or pinched at the tip (BlenderBottle scene 4, Liquid I.V. scene 1)

**Not fixed** · 2 ads, 2 talking clips (13 of 19 hold-to-lens pictures in the round used such grips)

- **Why it happened:** The picture form told the drawer to hold the product close to the lens with its label clear, and nothing said how a real person holds it, so it chose grips that keep the label uncovered. A label-size gate (removed during the round) made it enlarge those pinches. The picture check lets a slightly wrong hand pass on purpose.
- **What was changed:** Drawer rule: hold the product the way a real person would for its size (small items between thumb and two fingers, big ones in the whole hand), never on a fingertip, one edge or one finger; a natural grip beats a fully readable label. The label-size gate was removed earlier (#428). (#473)
- **What happened next:** Not merged yet; no test by Julian's choice.

### 8. Product drawn too small in one scene (KONG scene 2)

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The agent asked for the toy at about a third of the frame and the picture model drew it at 23% (a one-off model miss); nothing tells the agent the product's real size, and the page sells six sizes.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed (Julian called it a random picture-drawing glitch that is really hard to fix)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 9. Wrong product demo: KONG stuffed by pinching one kibble above the hole, not the page's method

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The page's how-to photos (mix, fill, seal, freeze) were tiny thumbnails the scene helper never opened; it invented the method.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed yet (proposal: open the page's how-to photos before drawing a use scene; not approved)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 10. Same product shown at different sizes across scenes (KONG); Julian asked for one size in every scene and a system that guarantees it

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 ad

- **Why it happened:** Each scene sets size only as a share of the frame; no plan-level size or reference photo is shared by all scenes.
- **What was changed:** The planner now picks the product photos (one version, one size) and writes the product's real size, read from the page or estimated and marked so. Every scene agent is told both at the top of its context, and code refuses a starting picture that picks none of the product photos. (PR: Keep scenes matching each other)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 11. Crocs held beside a dress instead of worn, with a made-up outfit

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The plan had feet walking in the clogs, but a fact-check rewrite was told 'B-roll shows hands and product only' and swapped in holding the clogs, copying a page photo's outfit. The agent never opened the page's worn-with-outfit photos.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed (Julian's idea: copy the outfit from the page photos instead of inventing one)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 12. Physics break: the heel strap passes through the leg (Crocs scenes 3 and 4)

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 2 clips

- **Why it happened:** The clip judge's motion questions were removed on Nov 4 because they wrongly failed most clips, so nothing asks about impossible motion; the clip description did not see it either. The ordered strap swing may itself be impossible with the foot inside.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed (bringing back a physics question was discussed, not decided)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 13. Unnatural pour: nothing moves except the falling powder (Liquid I.V. scene 2)

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The prompt asked for a still hand ('maintains its downward tilt') and the starting picture was already mid-pour.
- **What was changed:** No fix found.
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 14. Hair passes through the brush tool unnaturally (Revlon scene 3)

**Known limit** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **What was changed:** Recorded as a known limit (Julian: 'a natural limitation of the video model')
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 15. Video model invented a sprayer (Bissell scene 2)

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, 1 clip

- **Why it happened:** The scene was a 7 s still shot ('rests untouched'); with nothing to do, the video model fills spare time with an invented action. No scene planned the spray, so it was in no starting picture.
- **What was changed:** No fix found.
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 16. Product changes between scenes: Gorilla Glue's hook shows a different bottle from scenes 2-4

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 ad, 2 clips named

- **Why it happened:** Each scene's agent picks its own photos; scene 1 chose the 2 fl oz bottle, the rest the 8 fl oz. The planner never picks one version for the whole ad.
- **What was changed:** The planner picks the photos of one exact product (only the 8 oz bottle's, say) and every starting picture must pick one of them. The weakness is recorded in the known-limits list and the [design record for one AI helper per B-roll scene](../adr/0011-one-agent-per-broll-scene.md): cohesion between scene agents is best effort, weaker than Creatify's single agent; a producer review of the whole ad is still open. (PR: Keep scenes matching each other)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 17. Props and demo drift between B-roll scenes (Gorilla Glue: big board, then two small pieces with a C-clamp, then medium boards in a bar clamp)

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 ad, 3 clips

- **Why it happened:** The plan said only 'wooden piece(s)' and 'a clamp'; each scene helper invents its own props and sees nothing of the other scenes; nothing reviews the whole ad.
- **What was changed:** The planner describes an object shared by several scenes once, concretely, in the same words in every scene. When a scene agent changes its action, a quick check compares it with the other scenes (same product, size, shared objects, story order) and refuses a real mismatch, 3 times per scene agent run at most; after that a mismatch goes through with a developer notice. (PR: Keep scenes matching each other)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 18. Viewer can't tell what the product is: CATAN sold as if the story island were real

**Not fixed** · 1 ad, all 3 clips named generally

- **Why it happened:** The page is written in the game's story and never says 'board game'; nothing tells the planner or plan check to make clear what the product is.
- **What was changed:** Not fixed (proposal: a general rule to say plainly what the product is and show it as people use it, plus a 'would a new viewer know what this is?' question)
- **What happened next:** No later graded round has checked it yet.

### 19. Pictures fail too often (Julian, Nov 8: 'why the picture fail a lot, it is a problem... We need to fix the root cause'); Chemical Guys scene 2 used up its tries

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 52 of 132 pictures failed their check in the round (Chemical Guys scene 2: 6 of 8)

- **Why it happened:** Several causes: starting and end pictures shared 6 tries; a label-size gate; rules fighting (hide unclear print vs check wants the logo); 'No text.' ending every edit request; the check failing very tiny print; products small in their reference photos.
- **What was changed:** See the four entries below plus the label-size gate removal during the round (#428). (#470, #471, #472)
- **What happened next:** No graded round since.

### 20. A failed end picture got filmed because starting and end pictures shared 6 tries (Chemical Guys scene 2, cost a paid clip and a second run)

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 1 scene

- **Why it happened:** One counter of 6 tries per scene run; the starting picture used 5, the only end-picture try failed, and filming used it anyway.
- **What was changed:** Starting and end pictures now get 6 tries each; after 6 failed end pictures the last one is still filmed (the option Julian picked) with a developer notice. (#470, #469)
- **What happened next:** No graded round since.

### 21. The picture check failed pictures for very tiny print it misread (the word TRADITION called 'misspelled' on pictures 4446 and 4568; Julian: 'the judge was clearly making a mistake')

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 2 pictures in one scene (10 small-print fails in the round)

- **Why it happened:** The picture check had no exception for fine print, unlike the clip judge.
- **What was changed:** Picture check: very tiny letters don't fail; the brand name and main logo words must still match. (#472)
- **What happened next:** Re-run on round 12 inputs: picture 4568 went from fail 3 of 3 to pass 3 of 3; picture 4500 (logo words gone) still fails 3 of 3. No graded round since.

### 22. Picture edits dropped or scrambled the product's logo

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 20 of 52 failed pictures failed on printed words (logo left off 10, small print garbled 10)

- **Why it happened:** Every edit and end-picture request ended with 'No text.', which the picture model can read as 'draw no words'.
- **What was changed:** Removed 'No text.' from picture edit requests. (#471)
- **What happened next:** No test by Julian's choice; no graded round since.

### 23. Products small in their reference photo (a collage, a whole kit) come out with wrong print or shape

**Not fixed** · 10 of 20 print/logo fails came from photos where the product is small

- **Why it happened:** Reference photos are sent whole; the product can be a corner of a collage.
- **What was changed:** Tried, not built: added an AI close-up of each reference photo. On 49 round 12 tries, fails went from 28 to 25 (5 fixed, 2 broken), within luck for one redraw each.
- **What happened next:** No clear gain in the test.


### 24. End pictures and some edits were drawn without any product photo, so the label drifted

**Fixed (not tested yet)** · 6 of 20 print/logo fails (5 end pictures, Hypervolt edit 4448)

- **Why it happened:** An end picture was sent only its starting picture, and an edit only the agent's new picks. The picture model redrew the label without seeing the real one, while the check compared it with the product photos.
- **What was changed:** Every end picture and every edit now also gets the product photos the starting picture was drawn from, each with its "what to take" line. A new pick of the same photo replaces the old one; the room picture is left out. (#475)
- **What happened next:** Redrew the 6 pictures twice each with the photos added, judged by today's picture check: end pictures passed 8 of 10 draws (4 of 5 passed both; Revlon 4465 failed both, REVLON missing), edit 4448 passed 1 of 2. Today's check already passes 3 of the old pictures (4568, 4634, 4448); 4500 and 4603 went from fail to pass. Cost $0.91. No graded round since.
