# Where AI decides, and what checks it

Every model call goes through `backend/gateway/gateway.py` under a named purpose; `backend/gateway/catalog.py` says which model each purpose uses. Paths below are under `backend/`. As of 2026-10-29.

Pictures are always drawn on OpenAI's own API, even when Azure answers the text and vision calls. With no `OPENAI_API_KEY`, each picture fails and says so.

How a decision can be checked:

- **Eval**: a real model's answers scored against my grades, run by hand.
- **Judge**: another model checks the output inside the app.
- **Guard**: code refuses or caps a bad answer.
- **Prompt only**: the rule is just text in the prompt.

The tests in `tests/` run on a scripted fake model (`gateway/fake.py`), so they prove the wiring and the guards, never the quality of a real answer. Locked prompt wording is pinned in `tests/test_frozen_rules.py`; a pin stops the wording from drifting, it doesn't grade it.

Harm if a decision is quietly wrong: **broken ad** (the owner sees a bad ad), **paid remake** (the owner pays for a useless remake).

| Decision | Runs per ad | Harm if wrong | Checked today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which page text is this product's (`copy_page_text`, `jobs/work.py`) | 1 per page | Broken ad: another product's facts | Guard: code drops any sentence not found on the page. One-off eval: `docs/scraping-test/copy-test` (repo root) |
| Is the page one product, or is the owner's paste usable (`check_page`) | 1 per page | Broken ad, or a good page refused | **Nothing.** An "unreadable" answer asks the owner to paste the page text and photos (`agents/page_panel.py`) |
| Which photos and videos are this product's, which photos show a face, what each photo shows (`pick_photos`, `note_face`, `label_photo`) | 1 pick; 1 face check and 1 labelling call per photo the picker never saw | Broken ad: a look-alike, a stranger's face, another product's video | Guard: unknown photo numbers dropped; a failed face check counts as a face. One-off eval: `docs/scraping-test/ref-photo-test` (repo root). Face check and labelling call not measured; a failed labelling call leaves the photo out of the library with a red chat notice |
| What each product video shows and says (`read_video`, `transcribe_video`, `jobs/video_reader.py`) | 1 read and 1 transcript per video | Broken ad: a misheard claim, a wrong moment kept as a photo | **No eval.** Guard: a moment is kept only from a real frame near its time; a failed video is skipped with a red chat notice |
| The script: scenes, lines, what B-roll shows, or a question to the owner (`plan_ad`, `jobs/planning.py`) | 1, again after each answered question | Broken ad: false claims, scenes that don't sell, a use nobody showed | Eval: `broll-test/should_ask.py` (does it ask when it should, 8 cases). Judge: fact check. Guard: first scene talks, photo numbers exist, at most 5 pictures a scene. Prompt only: one action fills the clip; film a use only when the page shows how |
| Is each line true to the page (`fact_check`, `jobs/checks.py`) | 1 per round, again after each rewrite | Broken ad: false claims ship | **Not measured.** Guard: after 2 failed rewrites the owner is asked |
| Rewrite, shorten, lengthen lines (`rewrite_line`, `shorten_line`, `lengthen_line`, `shorten_script`) | Per scene: up to 2 rewrites, 2 or 3 shortenings, 2 lengthenings | Broken ad: facts lost, padding, a selling scene dropped | Judge: fact check re-runs. Guard: try limits; a B-roll line still too long becomes a talking scene; a talking line still too long goes to the owner |
| Presenter, voice, speech, word timings, music (`draw_person`, `design_voice`, `measure_voice`, `speak_line`, `transcribe_line`, `make_music`) | 1 each; speech 1 per line | Broken ad: odd presenter, bad delivery, cuts mid-word | **Nothing** on quality |
| Talking-scene start picture (`choose_starting_picture`, `make_starting_picture`) | 1 per talking scene | Broken ad: presenter holds the wrong product or colour | **No judge.** Guard: only photos of the ad's colour can be chosen |
| B-roll sent list and motion prompt (`write_first_send_prompt`, `jobs/cut_and_send.py`) | 1 per B-roll scene's first clip | Paid remake, or broken ad: impossible objects, a clip that sells nothing | Judge: clip judge. There is no B-roll start picture (#241): the clip is made from its sent list |
| Shared before-picture (`plan_before_picture`, `make_before_picture`) | 1 | Broken ad: every B-roll scene inherits a wrong setting | **No judge** |
| Owner's photo on a note: a clean picture of its one thing (`make_reference_photo_picture`, `jobs/reference_photos.py`) | 1 per photo, up to 2 redraws | Paid remake: the thing drawn wrong | **No eval.** Judge: picture judge (when quality checks are on, the default) checks for impossible objects, not for a true copy. Guard: the owner's raw photo never goes to the video model |
| Picture judge: is the start picture possible (`check_starting_picture`, `jobs/picture_check.py`) | 2 or 3 asks per picture | Paid remake: a clip of an impossible object, or needless redraws | Eval: `evals/calibrate_picture_check.py`, 62 cases: caught 6 of 10 kinds of wrong picture, failed 0 of 52 good ones. Not used by the app since the B-roll start picture went (#241); kept for the talking-scene start picture, which it doesn't judge yet |
| Video clips (`make_talking_clip` on HeyGen, `make_broll_clip` on Creatify) | 1 per scene, plus 1 per fixing-agent try | Broken ad: bad lips, impossible motion, wrong action | Judge: clip judge, B-roll only. Guard: every B-roll clip is made from its sent list, never a start picture; a B-roll line over 15 s is refused before anything is paid |
| Clip prompt from an owner's note (`rewrite_clip_prompt`) | 1 when a scene's first B-roll clip is made with a note | Paid remake that ignores the note | Judge: clip judge asks if the change was made |
| Clip judge: references kept, the product right, and impossible motion (`check_clip_references`, `check_clip_product`, `check_clip_motion`, `jobs/clip_judge.py`) | 3 checks x 1 ask per B-roll clip | Broken ad if it misses; paid tries if it fails a good clip | Guard: a check fails when its one answer does (one ask matched Julian as often as the old 3-ask vote, `docs/adr/0008-clip-judge-asks-once.md`). Graded on 45 round 6 clips it had never seen, when it checked sides and motion: it passed 5 clips I failed and failed 9 I passed |
| Fixing agent: owns a failed B-roll scene, or a scene the producer hands it an owner note on, until a try passes or its tries run out (`fix_scene`, `agents/fixing_agent.py`) | 0 when the judge passes and no note is about a clip; else up to 6 tries per fix | Paid tries that repeat the fault; a bad clip ships on a wrong judge pass | Judge: clip judge on every try; fact check on a new line. Guard: code refuses ban words and a 7th try; every try is a version the owner can pick; a give-up after a judge fail keeps the last try in the ad (until Oct 23 it turned the scene into a talking scene). Round 6: sent 18 scenes; 7 ended with a clip I passed, but for 4 of those the clip was already fine and the judge was wrong; 4 ended with a clip I failed; 4 gave up; 3 were cut off when the app restarted mid-run |
| Producer agent: picks the next tool for the whole session, before and after the first clip, and says what each change cost (`produce`, `agents/producer.py`; ADR 0010) | Every owner message, every finished step | Broken ad: a step skipped or done wrong; paid remake: a dumb expensive move (five clips made again for one word) | **No eval** (graded on the round-6 products after the merge). Guard: the hard list (`agents/hard_list.py`: a second product, an unapproved owner part, the video API's limits) and every other refusal comes back as a tool answer; pause makes every step tool answer "paused"; stale marks; the 100-calls-per-message cap. Per-ad page of every tool call with its why (`evals/agent_page.py`) |
| Look: what a picture, clip, version, library item or sent list shows, in words (`look`, `agents/look.py`) | When the producer asks | Paid remake: the producer acts on a wrong reading | **No eval.** It decides nothing; the producer reads its words. Same model as the producer |

Biggest blind spots today: the producer's choices after the first clip and the look's words have no eval yet. The fact check is not measured. Talking-scene start pictures and the before-picture have no judge. The video reader has no eval, yet the planner and the fact check treat what it keeps as the shop's own words. The clip judge still misses failures and fails good clips, and the fixing agent trusts it, so a wrong pass ships a bad clip.
