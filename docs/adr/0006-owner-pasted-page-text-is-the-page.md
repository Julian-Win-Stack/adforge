# Owner-pasted page text is the page

Some product pages can't be read: the reader is refused or blocked, or the page comes back as a
loading screen, cookie wall, bot check or error page (12 of 126 test pages, Oct 19). For those the
shop owner is the only source left, so the chat opens the page panel and asks them to paste the
page's text and attach its photos. Code runs the same junk check on the paste as on a page, then
keeps it as the page text: the planner, the fact check and the line checks read it exactly as they
read a page that was read.

This keeps 0002's two sources. The paste is the page, and the owner stands behind what they pasted.

## Considered options

- **Treat the paste as user-supplied facts.** Rejected: user-supplied facts skip the fact check, so a
  whole pasted page would put every claim in it into the ad unchecked, and the planner would have no
  page to plan from.

## Consequences

- The fact check checks the ad's lines against the owner's own paste. It catches a line that says
  more than the paste does, but not a paste that is wrong: the owner is right about what they pasted.
- The paste goes to the page path, never to the producer as a chat message. A page pasted into the
  normal chat isn't read as facts; the producer points the owner to the page panel.
