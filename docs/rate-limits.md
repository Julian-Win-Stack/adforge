## What this means for parallel runs (2026-10-20)
- Pictures: 20/min on OpenAI. An ad needs ~6 pictures (inferred), so 22 ads need ~130 pictures, ~7 min of picture time if spread evenly. Bursts wait in the shared app-side line before reaching OpenAI.
- Creatify: no known cap; everything fires at once.

`scripts/ad-runs/run.py` still starts ads 20 s apart; that spacing is no longer needed for Firecrawl. The app limits all three services across every ad and worker using Postgres: a durable row keeps each account's next allowed start, and connection-held advisory locks cap concurrency. A dead worker's connection returns its slots automatically. Starts are spread evenly at the configured per-minute pace.

Waiting does not spend a retry, create a failed step, or charge a call. Scene heartbeat threads keep running while admission waits. After `SERVICE_LIMIT_NOTICE_SECONDS` (default 120 s), one developer-only `service_limit_wait` notice per waiting call is kept with its job and sent to Sentry; the call keeps waiting. No warning is shown to the owner.

Retries remain a backup inside the line: each outside attempt takes admission again. The current shared retry delays are **5 s, 20 s, 60 s and 120 s**, each with up to **20% jitter**. A longer `Retry-After` is honored up to **300 s**. Firecrawl additionally keeps its existing six waits for a 429 (the service's `Retry-After` or stated delay, bounded to 1–120 s, otherwise 60 s), before the final attempt.
