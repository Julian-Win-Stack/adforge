# Limitations

What AdForge deliberately does not do yet. Don't build these without Julian's go.

- **B-roll doesn't show the result the voice claims.** The presenter can say "94% saw fewer wrinkles in 30 days" while the B-roll only shows the serum going on. Showing real results is next-level ad work, and we don't know yet whether it sells more. (Decided 2026-10-10.)
- **A run stopped by a failing start picture doesn't cancel work already running.** When a B-roll start picture fails its check 3 times, the run stops and the producer starts nothing new until the shop owner writes again, but other scenes' pictures, audio or clips already being made still finish and are paid for. (Picture judge build, 2026-10-10.)
- The start-picture judge checks the product and the objects it touches. It does not catch a wrong background object, such as a toilet that isn't a normal toilet (4 graded cases, 0 of 5 caught by every model tried, Oct 10).
- **No stop button for a fix.** A note about a scene whose redo is still being made waits for it: the producer says it will start as soon as the scene is finished, and starts then. Work already running always finishes and is paid for. (Route B step 1, Julian 2026-10-11 21:44.)
- **The producer never says a fix worked.** It can't see pictures or videos, so after a fix it shows the new ad and asks the shop owner to say whether it's fixed. A fix's new starting picture or clip is checked for the change (#131), but a pass is the judge's word, not proof. (Route B step 1, 2026-10-11; #131, 2026-10-13.)
- **No escalation on a repeated note.** When a fix didn't work and the shop owner notes the same scene again, the same step is made again with the new fix, never a bigger one. Decided to test in rounds 4 and 5 first. (Julian 2026-10-11 21:53.)
- **A new line remakes the scene without its earlier fixes.** When the shop owner changes what a scene says, its starting picture and clip are made again with no note, so a picture or movement fix they said yes to on that scene before is not carried over. The producer tells them so before they say yes. A new line too long for the scene is refused up front; only one whose real audio still runs too long for a clip is shortened by a model, and the producer tells the shop owner the new wording. (Route B step 1, #138, 2026-10-12.)
- **HeyGen ignores motion text on talking clips.** A talking scene's presenter can't be told to do an action (pick up the product, point, turn): HeyGen makes the talking clip from the line and picture and ignores any motion asked for. So an action in a talking scene is a "can't": a movement fix on a talking scene is refused, and only its starting picture or its line can change instead. (Tested 2026-10-11: one clip, Julian's grade "no"; runs/heygen-motion-test/.)
- **The shop owner's photos are for scenes that show the product only.** A photo sent with a note on a scene where the presenter talks is refused: the producer says it can't use photos there yet. (Owner photos in notes, D7, 2026-10-15.)
- **A photo can't change the person.** "Our presenter should look like her" is a can't-yet: the person is made once and a photo never changes how they look, and a clean picture drawn from a photo never copies a face. (Julian 2026-10-15 17:45.)
- **No example videos.** The shop owner can send photos of their own things with a note, never a video to copy. (Julian 2026-10-15, Q6.)
- **A photo of something other than the product, sent before the ad is planned, isn't added.** Before the plan every photo is a product photo, so the producer asks them to send it with a note on a scene once the first version is made. (Julian 2026-10-15 17:55.)
- **A photo can't ride on a new line.** A note that changes what a scene says and sends a photo is restated as two changes, or the photo is left out: a new line remakes the scene from scratch. (Owner photos in notes, 2026-10-15.)
- **In one fix with a new starting picture, a thing that only shows during the clip isn't checked.** The picture's check looks only at things drawn into it, and the clip's check asks about the shop owner's change only for clip fixes. (Owner photos review, 2026-10-15.)
