## Silent problems
- **The portrait was drawn holding an invented bottle** — the planner's `person_looks` ends "…and holding the serum bottle on camera", and `PORTRAIT_PROMPT` pastes it after "No text, logos or products in the picture", so the portrait prompt contradicts itself. The picture model then had to *remove* it: `[22:25:21] choose_starting_picture` scene 1 prompt: "Replace the bottle she is holding with the product from the second image" (same wording in scene 4). So the presenter shown to the shop owner at `[22:24:51]` holds a made-up product, and every scene picture starts from a swap rather than a clean portrait. Severity: med.
- **Scene 2's B-roll needs a picture the page and photos don't support, and nobody asked** — plan `[22:24:09]`: shows = "The presenter gently applies an even layer to her face with the bottle standing beside her." `PLAN_INSTRUCTIONS` says to *ask* "when a scene showing the product would need something neither the page, the photos nor the shop owner shows or says, such as what a serum looks like out of the bottle", and the skincare guide allows hands applying it "only if the page gives the texture". The page gives no texture (only "Apply an even layer to the face, avoiding the eye area"); the two photos are the capped bottle. The B-roll prompt `[22:25:21] choose_broll_picture` asks for her "gently applying an even layer to her face" with nothing about what is being applied, so the serum on skin is invented. `fact_check [22:25:02]` waved it through: "the application shown are supported by the product page". The 3rd try's planner avoided this ("neither available photo shows the dispenser or a pea-sized drop"). Severity: med.
- **A "30-second" ad that would have run about 23 s** — audio made: 7.08 + 5.54 + 7.16 + 4.98 = 24.76 s, and assembly trims each clip to its words, so about 23 s. `run_planning_checks [22:25:02]` said "the script fits your 30-second target" and the producer told the owner "make sure the script fits 30 seconds": `fits_target` only rejects running *over* (target + 1 s); nothing rejects running 7 s short. (known as a symptom in #24; the missing lower bound is not noted) Severity: low.
- **Photo 2 never used** — all four `choose_*` calls picked photo 1 ("clear, straight-on view"), so the second product photo the owner was told about ("two product photos") had no effect. Severity: low.
- **Producer echoes the attachment placeholder** — the app renders attachment-only agent messages to the producer as `[Attached 1 picture]` (`›conv[said/agent]` at `[22:25:52]`). Not echoed in this run, but in the 3rd try the producer copies it as its own reply (see that file); this is the mechanism behind the known "[Attached 1 picture]" messages. (known symptom) Severity: low.
- **Page text is ~60% duplicated navigation** — the 9.7k-char `page_text` sent to check_page, plan_ad and fact_check contains the site menu twice ("Skincare / Shop All / … Mandelic Acid" repeated) and banners ("15% OFF BODY WASH FOR A LIMITED TIME ONLY"). Nothing leaked into the plan, but every text call pays for it. Severity: low.
