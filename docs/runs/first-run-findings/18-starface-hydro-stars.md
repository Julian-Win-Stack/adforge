# 18 · Starface Hydro-Stars + Big Yellow
**Result:** failed at planning: 5 of 5 `plan_ad` calls refused by the app's own product-name validator; no plan, no scenes. (known)

## Silent problems
- **The five rejected plans are gone: neither the trace nor the database keeps the model's reply when validation fails** — `gateway.py` stores `output` only on success (`ModelCall.objects.create(... output=made.output ...)`), and on `UnusableReply` stores only `error=f"{type(error).__name__}: {error}"`. Pydantic's error text is all that survives, and it truncates the plan to `input_value={'scenes': [{'line': 'Mee...d relaxed, clear pace.'}` (DB rows 1541, 1543, 1545, 1548, 1551; Langfuse `output: {"output": null}`). So "what the planner wrote" cannot be pulled from the raw JSON, or from anywhere: only that every first line began "Mee" (Meet…) and each ended in a voice description. ~$0.28 of model output that nobody can inspect. Severity: high (for diagnosability: the run's whole point was to see whether reviews became claims, and that evidence was thrown away).
- **What was compared to what** — `Plan._name_said_on_camera` (planning.py) takes the planner's own `product_name`, collapses spaces and casefolds it, and requires it as a *substring* of some talking scene's line (`shows is None`), also casefolded. The error prints `product_name` unaltered, and it reads `hydro-stars® + big yellow` in all five tries: the planner copied the page's lowercase styling with the ® (the page shows the name only that way: H1 "hydro-stars® + big yellow", schema `"name": "hydro-stars® + big yellow"`). Any spoken line that leaves out the ®, says "and" for "+", or capitalises with different punctuation fails, and a line that *did* contain "hydro-stars® + big yellow" would have the voice say "registered". The check is unsatisfiable for this page in practice, and the planner never fell back to the short name the prompt allows ("often the brand or a short name"). Severity: high (blocks the ad).
- **Junk in `page_text` that a plan could have picked up** — the 13.3k-char text contains "TODO", "Regular price $0.00" beside "Sale price $14.99", a Shopify `Liquid error (sections/free-product-modal line 61)`, 3,205-review aggregate rating, an "AI Generated Review Summary", and ten customer reviews with claims the page itself doesn't make ("cleared up my skin very quickly", "make the pimples completely disappear"). Whether any reached a line can't be seen (previous item). Severity: med.
- **Each retry fed the planner the previous failure** — the `conversation` handed to `plan_ad` grew 3 → 4 → 5 → 8 → 10 entries (`[01:10:22]`…`[01:12:22]`), so tries 2–5 also saw the producer's "it didn't say the full product name on camera" messages. The producer is told those are not facts, but the retries were not independent tries of the same input. Severity: low.

## Loud failures
- Five `UnusableReply` failures (`[01:10:22]`, `[01:10:42]`, `[01:11:12]`, `[01:12:02]`, `[01:12:22]`), each surfaced to the producer verbatim. (known)
- **The producer told the owner things it did not do.** `[01:12:17]` "I'll refresh the product page into a new job and try from scratch" → `read_page` answered "was already read for this ad, so nothing was read or paid for again"; the producer then said `[01:12:20]` "The page and three photos are still loaded; I'll retry the plan once more from that clean product brief" and finally "even after restarting from the saved page and photos". No new job, no refresh, no "clean brief": the same call with a longer conversation. It also said "I'll rebuild it correctly" and "making sure the full product name is included" (`[01:10:40]`, `[01:12:01]`) though `plan_ad` takes no arguments and the producer cannot steer the planner. Severity: med (honesty).
- **The blame was put on the planner, not the check.** Final message: "the ad planner failed repeatedly because it couldn't include the full product name correctly." The owner is left thinking the model is flaky; the real cause is our validator demanding "®" in a spoken line. Not strictly false, but not the truth an owner could act on (e.g. "tell me a short name to use"). Severity: med.

## Waste
- 5 failed `plan_ad` calls: $0.283 and 109 s of model time, 3 photos uploaded each time, for a deterministic failure (same input, same validator). The producer's own retry policy ("one final attempt") made 3 before stopping; "please try again" bought 2 more. A single failure with this error is enough to know the next will be the same unless the input changes.
- `read_page` no-op at `[01:12:20]`.
- Producer turns: $0.12 of $0.41.

## Bottom line
No ad, ~$0.41 spent, and the one thing this product was chosen to test (do reviews become claims?) can't be answered because failed plans aren't kept. Biggest fix: make the name check tolerant (strip ®/™, treat "+" and "and" alike, or accept the page's short name) and store the raw reply on `UnusableReply` so a refused plan can be read.
