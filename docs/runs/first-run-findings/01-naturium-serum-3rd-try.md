# 01 · Naturium Multi-Active Exosome Serum (3rd try)
**Result:** finished ad, 28.66 s, 5 scenes (no target length asked); 1 failed `plan_ad` first. (known)

## Silent problems
- **45% of the ad is a still bottle, twice in a row** — scenes 2 and 3 are both B-roll of the same capped bottle standing centred in the same studio (`[01:24:40] choose_broll_picture` ×2: "close-up of the capped serum bottle … standing upright" / "clean product close-up of the capped serum bottle … upright in the middle of the frame"), both from photo 1, with only "slowly turns in place" vs "very slow, subtle handheld push-in" to tell them apart. Together they run 6.08→18.84 s = 12.76 s of the 28.66 s ad, straight after scene 1 in which she already holds that bottle. The skincare guide in `PLAN_INSTRUCTIONS` says about 30% B-roll showing "a texture close-up, hands applying it, the pack"; and scene 3's line is the proof claim ("94% saw a reduced look…") illustrated by the pack, i.e. mood not proof (docs/what-makes-a-good-ad.md, "Proof, not mood"). The first try's planner had the opposite problem (an unsupported application scene); this one over-corrected to two pack shots. Severity: med.
- **Photo 2 never used** — 5 of 5 `choose_*` calls picked photo 1; the picture chooser for scene 4 even said "neither available photo shows the dispenser or a pea-sized drop" and still had nothing else to pick. Same in the first try. Severity: low.
- **Portrait prompt asks for the bottle again** — `[01:23:44] draw_person`: "No text, logos or products in the picture. The person: … holding the metallic light blue bottle during direct-to-camera scenes." The same contradiction as the first try (there the scene prompts said "Replace the bottle she is holding"); here the scene prompts say "Place the product … in her hand", so this portrait may have come out clean, but it is chance. Severity: low.
- **Caption drops the hyphen in the closing line** — scene 5 was heard as "Multi Active Exosome Serum is $27" (`[01:24:46] transcribe_line`), so the last two captions read "Multi Active Exosome" / "Serum is $27" (DB `captions`), while scene 1's overlay and captions read "Multi-Active". The producer told the owner "All five voice recordings are verified and match the approved script" (`[01:24:49]`). Cosmetic. Severity: low.
- **Producer copies the attachment placeholder as its own message** — at `[01:25:11]` and `[01:25:19]` the producer's whole reply is `SAYS: "[Attached 1 picture]"`, imitating the `›conv[said/agent]: {"text": "[Attached 1 picture]"}` entries the app shows it for attachment-only messages. This is the cause of the known "[Attached 1 picture]" messages (#20 and #26 in first-run.md): the placeholder text is indistinguishable from something the producer said. (known symptom, cause new) Severity: low.
- **Timing checked, nothing wrong** — every clip was asked for its audio length or the next third-second (silent clips 6.5 s for 6.28 s audio, 7.5 s for 7.36 s; Boreal's floor(3s)/3 + 1/24 gives 6.375 and 7.375, both long enough, the second by 0.015 s). No last word within 0.1 s of a clip end (closest: scene 4, 6.8 of 7.28). Cuts in the DB add up to 28.66 s; music 34 s, trimmed. Facts: every line and overlay ("94% in 30 days", "AM + PM", "$27") is on the page; "daily" is "Can be used daily"; the page's "Sale price $27" is Shopify's label with no other price, so "$27" is right.

## Loud failures
- `[01:22:52] plan_ad` refused by the name check: `No scene where the person talks to camera says "Naturium Multi-Active Exosome Serum"`. The failed lines are not recorded (see the #18 file), but the accepted plan's line 1 is "This is Naturium’s Multi-Active Exosome Serum…" and the redo's was "Meet Naturium’s…": the likeliest cause is the possessive, since `naturium multi-active` is not a substring of `naturium's multi-active`. The producer said "The first draft missed the required spoken product name" — true in the check's terms, misleading to the owner (the name was almost certainly said). $0.065 and 32 s. (known as a symptom)

## Waste
- The failed plan: $0.0647, 32 s (the most expensive single text call in the run).
- `create_music` called alone and waited on (15.7 s) before any scene started (`[01:24:20]` → `[01:24:40]`); it could run beside the scene steps.
- 4 producer turns that only report "Scene N is finished … still rendering" (`[01:25:48]`–`[01:25:55]`); producer turns are $0.51 of $1.47.

## Bottom line
A usable, factually clean ad, but a shop owner would notice that almost half of it is a bottle standing still, twice, and the only "verified" claim it illustrates is the 94% study. Biggest fix: give the planner a rule against two B-rolls in a row on the same view (or cap B-roll share for skincare at the 30% its own guide says), so proof lines get the person, not the pack.
