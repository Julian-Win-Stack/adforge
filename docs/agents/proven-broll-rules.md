# Proven B-roll rules

Rules Julian's grades proved. Change one only with his explicit OK, showing old vs new logic and the grade behind it.

- Every B-roll scene shows proof of its line; a still product shot passes only when the line is about exactly that, such as where it fits or how it looks. The planner writes each scene's proof and the plan quality check sends back a plan whose action doesn't show it (replaced "B-roll must sell: every clip DOES something; holding or placing fails unless holding is the claim", Julian's OK on Nov 6 2026 at 06:14, spec #401).
- B-roll may show a result the page states in words, as the action's own result in the same clip, where the product touched; never a number shown as a result, and never a before and after over days (spec #401).
- Talking-scene exception for H3-voiced ads: showing the product to the lens counts as the action while she talks. Julian approved this on Nov 3 2026 at 20:24; both Creatify first versions he graded good opened and closed with her showing the product to the lens while talking (spec #327).
- Finishing steps (putting it away, wiping up) get no clip.
- One action per clip. A named movement the shop owner asked for beats the one-action rule, and an auto-redo keeps every step the owner asked for.
- A two-state claim is two one-action clips joined by a cut.
- Only "real-time speed" from the opener.
- Always draw a start picture; start and end pictures are orders the clip must follow.
- Photos are for looks only (a photo's pose gets copied).
- Never show a side or part no photo shows. (Widened from "never flip the product to a side no photo shows" with Julian's OK, Nov 6 2026 06:13.)
- A result shows only where the product touched.
- Problem scenes: nobody touches the product.
