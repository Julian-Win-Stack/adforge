# Proven B-roll rules

Rules Julian's grades proved. Change one only with his explicit OK, showing old vs new logic and the grade behind it.

- B-roll must sell. Every B-roll scene does something with the product that sells it: a hand only holding, placing, setting down, standing up or pointing at the product isn't B-roll, except a still shot of it when the line is about exactly where it fits or how it looks, or the product held next to its result on her body, for contents used on the body. The rule doesn't tie the line to the action, and no check asks it. Julian's rule from his round 2 grades (Oct 9 2026 02:15: three B-roll scenes failed where a hand only placed, stood or held the product). The proof rule ("every B-roll scene shows proof of its line", spec #401, Nov 6 2026) replaced it, with a written proof per scene and a plan check asking about it; Julian put the round 2 rule back on Nov 7 2026 at 17:56 UTC (#437) because the proof check sent back about half of all plans, mostly asking for proof no camera can show, such as "near-silent" or "it lubricates". The exception for the product held next to its result on her body came with Julian's OK on Nov 9 2026 at 22:54 (round 13, root cause 4 part 1): applying a cream or foam had never passed (Glow Recipe round 6, Krave rounds 8-10, Bondi rounds 12-13), because the video model makes up how it behaves. The old ladder allowed applying when a page photo or video showed it, then the result, then a fingertip dab, then talking; Julian passed that test on Nov 10 2026. He removed the applying step with his explicit OK on Nov 14 2026 at 20:58 UTC ([#653](https://github.com/Julian-Win-Stack/adforge/issues/653)), after baseline-2 Jones Road Miracle Balm scene 2: the ad passed, but H3 invented how the balm softened and melted while her fingers massaged it, something only a real owner could grade. The order is now the finished spot as a page photo shows it, then the contents coming out, then talking. The applied spot must look visibly different and be clearly framed, never pointed at; the free hand stays near it, never rubbing. For any product whose contents come out, talking and B-roll scenes show those contents coming out or finished, never changing, even when a page photo or video shows the change. One side per clip: starting and end pictures show the contents in the same state; if both are needed, use two B-roll scenes joined by a cut. Finished scenes move the product, glass or spoon, never the contents. Cleaners wiping or scrubbing marks off surfaces and drink or sauce pours stay allowed. The shared `jobs/planning.py:CONTENTS_RULE` reaches every scene kind through `EVERY_SCENE_RULES`; it is told, never checked.
- B-roll may show a result the page states in words, where the product touched (spec #401). The same-clip, number and before-and-after-over-days limits were removed with Julian's OK (Nov 9 2026, 00:05-00:06): no graded failure was behind them.
- Talking-scene exception for H3-voiced ads: showing the product to the lens counts as the action while she talks, in the first or the last scene only. Julian approved this on Nov 3 2026 at 20:24; both Creatify first versions he graded good opened and closed with her showing the product to the lens while talking (spec #327). He narrowed it to the first and last scenes on Nov 6 2026 at 05:59 (spec #401, ticket #403, [ADR 0013](../adr/0013-more-broll-whole-clips.md)): 25 of round 11's 28 talking scenes only held the product to the lens. Since Nov 9 2026 it is told, never checked (rules review): a change to one scene after planning that breaks it goes ahead with a developer notice (`plan_rule_let_through`, rule 1, 00:00: round 12's KONG scene 3 was lost when that fallback was refused), and so does a new plan (`plan_rule_not_kept`, 00:15: it never refused a plan in round 12). The scene agent is told it may show the product to the lens as its last fallback in any scene (Julian's OK, Nov 9 2026 00:34).
- Finishing steps (putting it away, wiping up) get no clip.
- One product action per clip: an action that moves the product or changes its shape or state. Face, head, free-hand and camera moves don't count. A named movement the shop owner asked for beats the one-action rule, and an auto-redo keeps every step the owner asked for. (Narrowed to product actions with Julian's OK, Nov 8 2026 20:59: the fails behind the rule were product steps, the Oct 6 bag and the Oct 8 curler, both on Boreal-H3; Creatify's agent gives the same rule, Rule 1 of its action-count answer, Nov 8.)
- A two-state claim is two one-action clips joined by a cut.
- Only "real-time speed" from the opener.
- Always draw a start picture; start and end pictures are orders the clip must follow.
- Photos are for looks only (a photo's pose gets copied).
- Never show a side or part no photo shows. (Widened from "never flip the product to a side no photo shows" with Julian's OK, Nov 6 2026 06:13.) A drawn part's own picture is the picture that shows it; Julian approved filming it without the owner's yes on Nov 15 2026 at 06:20 UTC ([#662](https://github.com/Julian-Win-Stack/adforge/issues/662)).
- A result shows only where the product touched. The round 2 rule "a result is filmed happening, from before to after" (a finished thing with the product beside it never shows a result) has one exception, with Julian's OK on Nov 9 2026 at 22:54 for the same reason: a result on her body that a page photo shows, where the product touched. Before and after pictures of bodies or skin stay banned.
- Problem scenes: nobody touches the product.
