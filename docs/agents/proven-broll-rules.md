# Proven B-roll rules

Rules Julian's grades proved. Change one only with his explicit OK, showing old vs new logic and the grade behind it.

- B-roll must sell. Every B-roll scene does something with the product that sells it: a hand only holding, placing, setting down, standing up or pointing at the product isn't B-roll, except a still shot of it when the line is about exactly where it fits or how it looks. The rule doesn't tie the line to the action, and no check asks it. Julian's rule from his round 2 grades (Oct 9 2026 02:15: three B-roll scenes failed where a hand only placed, stood or held the product). The proof rule ("every B-roll scene shows proof of its line", spec #401, Nov 6 2026) replaced it, with a written proof per scene and a plan check asking about it; Julian put the round 2 rule back on Nov 7 2026 at 17:56 UTC (#437) because the proof check sent back about half of all plans, mostly asking for proof no camera can show, such as "near-silent" or "it lubricates".
- B-roll may show a result the page states in words, as the action's own result in the same clip, where the product touched; never a number shown as a result, and never a before and after over days (spec #401).
- Talking-scene exception for H3-voiced ads: showing the product to the lens counts as the action while she talks, in the first or the last scene only. Julian approved this on Nov 3 2026 at 20:24; both Creatify first versions he graded good opened and closed with her showing the product to the lens while talking (spec #327). He narrowed it to the first and last scenes on Nov 6 2026 at 05:59 (spec #401, ticket #403, [ADR 0013](../adr/0013-more-broll-whole-clips.md)): 25 of round 11's 28 talking scenes only held the product to the lens. Since Nov 9 2026 00:00 (Julian's OK, rules review rule 1) only the planner is held to it: a change to one scene after planning that shows the product to the lens in a middle scene goes ahead with a developer notice (`plan_rule_let_through`), because round 12's KONG scene 3 was lost when that fallback was refused. The scene agent is told it may show the product to the lens as its last fallback in any scene (Julian's OK, Nov 9 2026 00:34).
- Finishing steps (putting it away, wiping up) get no clip.
- One product action per clip: an action that moves the product or changes its shape or state. Face, head, free-hand and camera moves don't count. A named movement the shop owner asked for beats the one-action rule, and an auto-redo keeps every step the owner asked for. (Narrowed to product actions with Julian's OK, Nov 8 2026 20:59: the fails behind the rule were product steps, the Oct 6 bag and the Oct 8 curler, both on Boreal-H3; Creatify's agent gives the same rule, Rule 1 of its action-count answer, Nov 8.)
- A two-state claim is two one-action clips joined by a cut.
- Only "real-time speed" from the opener.
- Always draw a start picture; start and end pictures are orders the clip must follow.
- Photos are for looks only (a photo's pose gets copied).
- Never show a side or part no photo shows. (Widened from "never flip the product to a side no photo shows" with Julian's OK, Nov 6 2026 06:13.)
- A result shows only where the product touched.
- Problem scenes: nobody touches the product.
